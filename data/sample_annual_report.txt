$482.6 million, up 12.4% from $429.4 million in fiscal 2023. Growth was led by our direct-to-consumer

supply-chain resilience while returning capital to shareholders through dividends and buybacks.

RISK FACTORS
Our results depend on consumer discretionary spending, which can decline in economic downturns.
Approximately 62% of our products are manufactured in Southeast Asia; tariffs, shipping disruptions
or currency fluctuations could raise costs. Our business is seasonal, with roughly 38% of annual
revenue earned in the fourth quarter. Unfavourable weather can reduce demand for seasonal products.
We rely on a small number of wholesale partners; our largest partner accounted for 14% of revenue.

OUTLOOK FOR FISCAL 2025
